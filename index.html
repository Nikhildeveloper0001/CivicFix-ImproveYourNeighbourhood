How CivicFix Works

Three simple steps to help identify local problems.

1. Report

Describe the problem, add its location, and attach a photograph if available.

2. Track

Find your report in the issue list and follow its displayed status.

3. Verify

Check whether a reported problem appears to have been resolved.
